Input: light-field camera grid, candidate disparity values
1. Extend the focus candidate range on both sides by the symmetry window.
2. Refocus the light field at all these disparities by shift-and-average.
3. For each pixel and candidate disparity:
   - compute ordinary symmetry and centered antisymmetry costs.
   - compute robust color+gradient camera correspondence costs.
   - predict the focal profile from the center camera, initially assuming equal local disparities.
4. Estimate an occlusion probability from the three paper measurements.
5. Combine normal/antisymmetric scores using the occlusion probability.
6. Estimate the initial disparity from the combined data costs.
7. Repeat:
   - re-render the predicted local focal profiles using the current disparity map.
   - recompute the data cost and disparity estimates.
8. Minimize combined data and spatial smoothness with alpha-beta swap graph cuts.
9. Save disparity and occlusion maps; compute evaluation errors using held-out ground truth.
